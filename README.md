# Exploring Careers in Data Analytics

**A small educational project demonstrating structured data collection, validation, and exploratory data analysis.**

> **Dataset disclosure:** All 25 records in this repository are **synthetic/test responses**, submitted programmatically through Google Forms. They do **not** represent a survey of 25 real participants. Results illustrate analysis methods, not real-world attitudes or behavior.

## Project overview

**Question:** How can professional backgrounds, learning motivations, and study preferences be captured and explored in a structured dataset?

I designed a 15-question Google Form to capture demographic and professional background, familiarity with data analytics, motivations, study availability, learning preferences, and perceived obstacles. I linked the form to Google Sheets and used 25 fictional respondent profiles to test the collection pipeline. I then exported the resulting dataset as CSV and used Python/Pandas to summarize and visualize it.

## Tools and methods

- **Google Forms:** Data collection; required versus optional fields, multiple choice, dropdowns, checkboxes, and numeric-response validation.
- **Google Sheets:** Automatic collection of form submissions and inspection of records.
- **Python / Pandas:** Descriptive statistics and group comparisons.
- **Matplotlib:** Bar charts for categorical frequencies and mean weekly study hours by employment status.

## Dataset

- **25 synthetic records**, one per simulated submission
- **16 columns**, including the Google Forms submission timestamp and 15 question fields
- **Data types:** Numeric, categorical, ordinal, multi-select, and optional free text
- **Missing answers:** Optional questions were intentionally left blank for some synthetic records.

File: [`data/synthetic_survey_responses.csv`](data/synthetic_survey_responses.csv)

**Important interpretation note:** Google Forms responses labeled `None` in the tools question mean *no tools used*; they must not be treated as missing data. The analysis reads the CSV with `keep_default_na=False` for this reason.

## Illustrative findings (synthetic data only)

- **8 of 25** fictional profiles selected *Changing careers* as their primary motivation.
- **7 of 25** selected *Lack of time* as their biggest obstacle.
- Mean planned learning time was **9.12 hours/week** across the 25 synthetic profiles.

These outcomes are generated by the profile design. They do **not** establish patterns among actual prospective data analysts.

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python analysis.py
```

This prints summary statistics and recreates all three figures in `charts/`.

## Limitations and next steps

The sample is small and fully simulated, with intentionally designed combinations of respondent characteristics. Statistical inference, significance tests, and generalizations to real populations would be inappropriate. As a next step, a future version could collect consenting real participants, document the recruitment method, and analyze results separately from these test records.
